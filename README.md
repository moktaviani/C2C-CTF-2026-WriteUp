## 🌍 Country-to-Country CTF (C2C) Cybersecurity Challenge

The **Country-to-Country (C2C) Cybersecurity Challenge** is a global annual Capture The Flag competition organized by the **International Cyber Security – Center of Excellence (INCS-CoE)** in collaboration with leading international universities around the world.

C2C CTF promotes:
- International collaboration in cybersecurity
- Technical skill development
- Teamwork and problem-solving
- Knowledge sharing between students worldwide
- Friendship and cultural exchange through cyber competitions

The competition brings together university students from different countries to solve real-world cybersecurity challenges in various domains such as:
- Web Exploitation
- Reverse Engineering
- Cryptography
- Digital Forensics
- Binary Exploitation (Pwn)
- Blockchain
- Miscellaneous Challenges
